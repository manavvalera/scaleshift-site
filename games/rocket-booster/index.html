Strap in. Blast off. Don't crash.

Rocket Booster is a fast, fun and slightly crazy rocket-flying game. Launch from the pad, dodge spinning gears, moving obstacles and falling fire rocks, grab every coin on the way, and stick the landing on the landing pad. Sounds easy? Wait till you meet the slow-down zones!

How to play

Just 3 buttons. Hold the thrust button on the right to blast upward, and use the two buttons on the left to steer your rocket left and right. Easy to learn, hard to master!

Quick answers

What is Rocket Booster?

A fast, fun and slightly crazy rocket-flying game where you launch from the pad, dodge hazards, collect coins and land safely on the landing pad.

Which platform is it on?

Rocket Booster is made for Android and is coming soon to Google Play. Follow us on Instagram to know the moment it launches.

How do the controls work?

There are 3 buttons: hold the thrust button on the right to fly upward, and use the two buttons on the left to steer left and right.

How many levels are there?

There are 10 handcrafted levels, and more levels are coming soon.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You do not need to create an account or log in. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Who makes Rocket Booster?

Rocket Booster is made by Scale Shift Studio, a game and software studio based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India.
